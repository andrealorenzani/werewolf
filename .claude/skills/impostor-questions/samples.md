# The 21 reference pairs (written by the maintainer)

These are the gold standard. They are pairs 1-21 of the pool, verbatim (one typo fixed: "westler"), and
**must always be present, in this order, as pairs 1-21** whenever the pool is regenerated or extended.
Every new pair is judged against them.

1. How would you like to die?
1-impostor. What disease would you least want to get?

2. What song do you want played at your funeral?
2-impostor. What song would you make love to?

3. What's your most important trait in a romantic partner?
3-impostor. What is the legal age of drinking in your country?

4. What is the first thing you'd do after getting out of prison?
4-impostor. If you find out the world was ending in one hour, what would you do?

5. What's your biggest turn off?
5-impostor. What's your biggest turn on?

6. How old were you when you had your first kiss?
6-impostor. How old were you when you lost your first baby thoot?

7. Who is your hall pass?
7-impostor. Who is the best but ugliest singer alive?

8. If you had to give your genitals a nickname, what would it be?
8-impostor. If you were a little person, pro wrestler, what would your stage name be?

9. How many times have you shit yourself as an adult?
9-impostor. How many times per week do you shower?

10. What is your favorite conspiracy theory?
10-impostor. Name a disaster that had a lot of deaths

11. Where on earth would you like to go on a content making holiday?
11-impostor. Which international destination is like Hell on earth for you?

12. How much money would you need to retire at your age?
12-impostor. What is the average wage in your home country?

13. What is the longest you have ever gone without changing your undies?
13-impostor. What's the longest time you've gone without a shower?

14. Which other player has the juiciest secret you know?
14-impostor. Who is the hottest of the players?

15. What is the worst intrusive thought you have ever had?
15-impostor. What is the most horrific crime you could do to another person?

16. *Bang!!!* Another player has been killed. If you could choose anybody in the world to replace him in the game, who would it be?
16-impostor. Name an Italian public character of your choice

17. Where is the best place for a first date?
17-impostor. Where is the worst place to be with your pants down?

18. Rate your kissing out of 10
18-impostor. How many fingers could you fit in your mouth

19. What is something you judge other people for?
19-impostor. What's something you worry other people judge you for?

20. Write a famous quote
20-impostor. If you could be remembered for one sentence, what would it be?

21. If you could have one wish granted right now, what would it be?
21-impostor. What could be able to end human civilisation?

# What makes them work (analysis)

**The comedy happens at the read-out, not in the question.** Everybody's answer is read aloud together. The Impostor
answers a different question *honestly*, and the answer lands in the wrong place:
- Q3 "most important trait in a partner" → the Impostor says "21" or "18". Everyone else says "honesty" or "humour".
- Q18 "rate your kissing /10" → the Impostor's answer to "how many fingers in your mouth" is "3" or "all of them".
- Q9 "times you shit yourself" vs "showers per week": two numbers, one is a confession and the other is nothing.
- Q16 "who replaces the dead player?" vs "an Italian public character" → a name that sounds stranger than the rest.

**1. Answers have the same *shape*.** A number, a duration, a name, a place, a song, a nickname, a one-liner. That is
what lets the Impostor bluff (3, 6, 9, 12, 13, 14, 16, 17, 18). If the shapes differ ("a number" vs "a name") the
Impostor is exposed immediately and the turn is boring.

**2. Sometimes the shape matches but the *register* does not.** The group answers something intimate, taboo or
embarrassing; the Impostor's question is bland (3, 9, 12, 13, 17-impostor in reverse). The Impostor then has to guess
how spicy to be: too tame is suspicious, too wild is also suspicious. Other times it is reversed: the group's question is
neutral and the Impostor's is dark (10, 15, 21). Both directions are good; vary them.

**3. Mirrors and flips** are the safest pairs and make perfect decoys: turn off/on (5), judge others / worry others
judge you (19), best place / worst place (17), best / ugliest singer (7). One word changes and the answers overlap
a lot, so players really have to read the room.

**4. Taboo topics, said lightly:** bodily functions (9, 13, 6 "baby thoot"), sex and kissing (2, 5, 8, 18), death
(1, 2, 10, 15, 21), prison (4), conspiracy theories (10), hygiene (13). The question dares the player to confess;
the group's reaction is the real joke.

**5. Silly words as comic fuel:** "baby thoot", "hall pass", "undies", "pro wrestler stage name", "genitals nickname".
A ridiculous noun makes everyone play along.

**6. Social targets:** several questions point at the *other players* (14 juiciest secret / hottest player, 16
replace the dead player). They make the vote personal and funny, because players answer with each other's names.

**7. Open creative prompts** ("Write a famous quote", "one sentence you want to be remembered for", "nickname",
"stage name") produce answers that are funny in themselves and easy to fake.

**8. The Impostor's question sometimes carries a trap:** it contains a fact that is easy to answer but gives itself
away ("legal drinking age" → a number that is wrong for a partner trait; "Italian public character" → a nationality
the others did not get).

**9. Unfiltered tone, never cruel to a group:** crude, adult, mildly shocking; but the jokes are about the players
themselves, the human condition, or silly things. Nothing hateful, nothing targeting a minority or a private person.

**Self-test for a new pair** (all should be "yes"):
1. Can both questions be answered in under ~10 words (the answer box is 100 characters)?
2. Do the two answers have the same shape?
3. Would the Impostor's honest answer be funny or suspicious next to the others?
4. Would the normal question make somebody laugh, blush or hesitate before answering?
5. Is it different in mechanism from the pairs next to it (number / name / place / phrase / duration)?
